A field guide for advertising students

From chatting with AI to running your own build team

You already use Claude or ChatGPT to write and brainstorm. This guide takes you from there to directing a set of AI agents that build working apps and workflows for you. No coding background is assumed.

21 modules, 5 phases
Go in order. Each one builds on the last.
One paid plan
An entry-level Claude plan. Free tiers for everything else.
Mac and Windows
Pick your system at the top of any page. The steps change to match.

How to use this guide

01
Every module has the same shape
What it is, why you need it, the steps, the common errors, and a short "you're done when" check at the end.
02
We link out instead of repeating
Where a tool already has a good getting-started video or walkthrough, the module sends you there and tells you what to pay attention to.
03
Short videos explain the ideas
Each phase has animated explainers for the concepts that are easier to see than read, like what a harness is.
04
You get real files to copy
Starter runbooks, agent definitions and prompts taken from a working setup, with private details removed.
